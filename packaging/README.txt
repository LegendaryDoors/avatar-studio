Avatar Studio
=============

Make, change and keep Xbox 360 avatars on your PC, and play as them in avatar games.

Start it: unzip the download first (right-click it, Extract All), then double-click
AvatarStudio.exe in the folder. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info,
then Run anyway (the studio is not signed; signing costs money and this project is free).

The first time, it gets the Xbox 360 files it needs by itself (about 200 MB, from the
Internet Archive, Microsoft's own avatar server and GitHub) and sets itself up. That
takes a few minutes; you don't need to do anything. If it stops (no internet, or you
pressed B), choose Try Again.

Controls: a controller, or the mouse and keyboard. The buttons shown at the bottom of
the screen change to keys as soon as you use the keyboard or mouse.
  Arrow keys        move around; at the edge of a page they turn to the next one
  Enter             choose            Esc               go back
  Page Up / Down    turn the page     Q / E             turn your avatar
  X, Y              the X and Y buttons shown at the bottom
  Mouse             point and click; right-click goes back; the wheel turns pages

Your avatars are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Avatar Studio\avatars, each with its saved
outfits beside it. Save and Exit keeps your changes, and so does closing the window.

To play as one of your avatars in your games, press X on its card (Use in Games). Its
card then says In game.

Avatar Studio is a fan-made preservation project. It contains no files of Microsoft's:
it downloads them onto your own PC from where they are already published. The parts it
is built with are open source; their licences are in the licenses folder.
